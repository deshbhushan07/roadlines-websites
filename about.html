Deshbhushan & Rushikesh Roadlines (DRRL)

Deshbhushan & Rushikesh Roadlines is a local transport company based in Sangavdewadi, Kolhapur district. For more than 10 years we have been providing dependable transport services to farmers, traders and industries in and around Kolhapur.

We started our journey by transporting rice, wheat, fruits and packaging boxes, and also supported farmers by moving sugarcane from farms to factories. Over time, with consistent service and on-time delivery, we built strong relationships with local companies and regular parties.

Today DRRL is well known for transporting paper rolls and industrial goods from Kagal MIDC to nearby areas such as Sangli, Ichalkaranji, Shiroli MIDC and other locations within a 70–80 km radius. With our well-maintained trucks (22 ft and 19 ft body) we can safely carry loads up to 12 tonnes.

Why customers trust DRRL

  • 10+ years of experience in local and MIDC transport
  • Special handling for paper rolls and sensitive cargo
  • Capacity of up to 12 ton with 22 ft & 19 ft trucks
  • Well connected with companies and regular parties in Kagal & nearby MIDCs
  • Safe loading, careful driving and timely delivery
  • Transparent pricing and reliable communication

Our mission is to deliver every load safely, on time and at a fair cost. Whether you need a single trip or regular transport for your factory or business, we are ready to serve you with professional and friendly service.
